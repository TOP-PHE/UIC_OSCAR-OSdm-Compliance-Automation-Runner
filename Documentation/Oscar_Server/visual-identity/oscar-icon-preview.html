Icon Design Rationale

⬤
UIC Blue circle background Anchors the icon firmly within the UIC / OSDM visual identity using the official primary blue (#0090D4 gradient), with a radial highlight for depth.
⚙
16-tooth gear ring (outer edge) White rounded rectangles at 22.5° intervals form a subtle cog ring — a universal symbol of automation and tooling, reinforcing Runner.
🛤
Perspective railway tracks (background) Very faint converging track lines and sleepers embed the OSDM / railway context without competing with the focal elements.
↻
Circular refresh arrows Two opposing semi-circular arcs with arrowheads suggest a continuous CI/CD-style test loop — OSCAR runs automatically, repeatedly.
★
Gold 5-pointed star (UIC Amber #FCC44D) The centrepiece plays on the Oscar award trophy. The amber-gold colour is the UIC 2024 accent4 — excellence and quality assurance.
✓
Bold white checkmark Overlaid on the star, the checkmark is the most direct symbol of Conformance — the C in OSCAR. Rounded ends add friendliness.
OSCAR
OSCAR wordmark badge A semi-transparent pill badge at the bottom carries the name in Arial Black — matching the UIC major font. Can be omitted for small sizes.

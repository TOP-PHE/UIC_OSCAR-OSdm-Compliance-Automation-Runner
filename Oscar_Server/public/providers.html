🔀 Providers

What a provider is

A provider is one OSDM system your company tests: your own, or a carrier you distribute. Each provider has its own endpoint, data file, Test Framework, findings, runs and reports, and each tester keeps separate credentials for it. Test Managers can use every provider; a tester uses the ones you grant below. Pick the provider to work on with the Working on selector in the menu bar.

🔀 Providers

What a provider is

A provider is one OSDM system your company tests: your own, or a carrier you distribute. Each provider has its own endpoint, data file, Test Framework, findings, runs and reports, and each tester keeps separate credentials for it. Test Managers can use every provider; a tester uses the ones you grant below. Pick the provider to work on with the Working on selector in the menu bar.

Company that operates this provider

If the company that runs this OSDM system has its own account in OSCAR, link it here to share chosen runs with its Test Managers (Dashboard, “Share with”). They accept the link first; they then read the runs you share, without changing anything, and see your company’s name instead of your testers’ e-mails. A provider without such a company needs no link.

Companies that share runs with you

These companies test your OSDM system as one of their providers and asked to share runs with you. Once you accept, the runs they share appear on your Dashboard, read only.

Testers with access

Test Managers always have access. Tick the testers who may use this provider.

🤖 Company API Accounts

What a Company API account is

A Company API account is the user your company's own test automation acts as when it asks OSCAR for tests through the API: a CI pipeline, a test tool. It has its own rights and its own OSDM credentials, and it never acts as a person. It never signs in to these pages and has no password. It tests your company and the providers you give it, and its runs are read by your company's Test Managers and testers. Suspend it to stop it at once; deleting it deletes its runs.

Signing in to the API

An account signs in with OAuth 2.0 client credentials and private_key_jwt: your company makes a key pair, keeps the private key where its CI keeps its secrets, and gives OSCAR the public key here. At each token request the CI signs a one-minute assertion with the private key and sends it to the token endpoint below. OSCAR never sees the private key. EC P-256 (ES256) or RSA of 2048 bits or more (PS256); two keys at once, to change one without a stop. For example: openssl ecparam -name prime256v1 -genkey -noout -out oscar-ci.key, then openssl ec -in oscar-ci.key -pubout -out oscar-ci.pub, and paste oscar-ci.pub.

What a Company API account is

A Company API account is the user your company's own test automation acts as when it asks OSCAR for tests through the API: a CI pipeline, a test tool. It has its own rights and its own OSDM credentials, and it never acts as a person. It never signs in to these pages and has no password. It tests your company and the providers you give it, and its runs are read by your company's Test Managers and testers. Suspend it to stop it at once; deleting it deletes its runs.

🧪 OSDM Simulator

Connection

OSCAR manages the simulator's accounts by calling its administration API with a key. The key is drawn on the simulator's host (openssl rand -hex 32), which keeps only its SHA-256 in SIM_ADMIN_KEY_SHA256. OSCAR keeps it encrypted and never shows it again. Address and key are tried before they are saved.

Accounts Statistics Audit

One account per OSCAR account keeps their bookings apart. A new secret is shown once: copy it then. A deleted account, and the old secret of a reset one, stop working at once; the other accounts and their bookings stay.

What each account did on the simulator, counted by the simulator itself: tokens asked, OSDM requests, offers asked, bookings made and confirmed, refunds and exchanges confirmed, answers in error. Requests whose account is not known (no token, a refused one) are counted on the provider's line.
